# Data sources

Every resistance figure in the app, the README, and the pitch deck cites from
this list. Do not cite a statistic from memory. If a number is not traceable to
a source here, it does not ship.

## Primary sources

1. **CDC AR and Patient Safety Portal**
   NHSN resistance data for 29 pathogen and antibiotic combinations across US
   hospitals. Used for national baseline resistance rates.

2. **WHO Global Antibiotic Resistance Surveillance Report 2025**
   Global and regional resistance trends for priority pathogens. Used for the
   six WHO region entries in the regional dataset.

3. **State antibiogram programs**
   Washington State DOH, Minnesota DOH, Colorado CDPHE, and NYC DOHMH each
   publish annual aggregated hospital antibiograms. Used for state level
   resistance rates.

4. **GRAM Project, Lancet 2024**
   "Global burden of bacterial antimicrobial resistance 1990 to 2021: a
   systematic analysis with forecasts to 2050". Source of the 39 million deaths
   figure and the age stratified trends.

5. **CDC Antibiotic Resistance Threats Report 2021 to 2022**
   US specific burden data. Source of the 2.8 million infections per year
   figure.

6. **IDSA 2024 Guidance on Treatment of Antimicrobial Resistant Gram Negative
   Infections**
   Clinical treatment guidelines. Informs the first line empiric antibiotic
   lists and the "what doctors consider" framing.

7. **Published risk factor studies**
   UTI resistance risk factors (Balfour et al. 2022), diabetes and AMR
   (systematic review, PMC8666814), travel and UTI resistance (PMC12118056),
   repeat UTI resistance (CIDRAP). These ground the weights in the risk model.

## Citation rules

- Every resistance statistic displayed in the app shows its source, its year,
  and its geographic scope.
- Label clearly when a figure is a national estimate standing in for missing
  state data. Never present a synthesized figure as facility specific.
- Note where relevant that antibiograms reflect hospital isolates, which skew
  toward sicker patients, and may overestimate community resistance rates.
- Antibiograms publish annually with a one to two year lag. Show the data year,
  not the current year.
